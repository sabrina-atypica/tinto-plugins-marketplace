## Cover copy

An insider's food and wine tour of Southern Tuscany & Umbria, from our base in the hilltop
town of Montepulciano. 6 nights based in stunning accommodations in the heart of
Montepulciano.

Medieval, hilltop towns with breathtaking views. Sipping some of the best wines Italy has to
offer while overlooking the Tuscan countryside. Tuscan food at its finest. We will visit the
following wine regions: Orvieto, Vino Nobile di Montepulciano, Brunello di Montalcino and
Chianti Classico.

We will be based in a splendidly restored Palazzo in the exceptional town of Montepulciano
for our 6 nights. Each day, we will venture out for wine, food and a little bit of culture. At
night, you are on your own to wander the quiet streets of Montepulciano, watch the sunset over
the Tuscan hills or just continue your eating and drinking adventure.

Pick up in Rome and drop off in Florence. Please plan to arrive in Rome no later than the
Sunday prior to the tour. All the flights to Rome are overnight flights, so plan on flying out
of the United States no later than Saturday.

We think Rome is best explored on your own or in very small groups. We have spent plenty of
time in Rome in the last few years and are happy to assist with hotel recommendations and
things to see and do.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: Pick up in Rome

We will meet you at 10am at Eataly, an easy meet up point near the Ostiense train station. The
location is approximately 15 to 20 minutes from central Rome.

We will have great wines featuring the Orvieto doc and a fantastic lunch outdoors with
Giovanni in Orvieto before heading to see the Duomo and wandering the charming streets of
the town for a bit.

We will arrive in the late afternoon and check in to our rooms in the heart of Montepulciano.

Our tour has its own daily rhythm for days 2 through 6. Each day consists of a nice relaxed
(not too early) breakfast before we head out for a bit of culture, a wine tasting and a long
leisurely lunch or dinner. In the evening, you can explore the beauty and stillness of
Montepulciano. If you are full from today's lunch, just grab a glass of wine and perhaps a
bruschetta from a nearby wine bar. Still hungry? Among the 50 or so restaurants in town, there
are at least a dozen restaurants in Montepulciano we enjoy.

### Day 2: After the drive up from Rome yesterday, today's schedule is easy

A little culture: you will get to know your base of Montepulciano, its history, cool shopping,
views and ancient Etruscan wine cellars.

Eating: we are going to have a lunch at one of our favorite restaurants in Montepulciano. The
setting is intimate and elegant and the food and wine pair perfectly with this ambiance.

Wine tasting: we will visit Olimpia and her picture-perfect winery called Le Bertille, a very
small producer of Vino Nobile, just down the hill from Montepulciano.

### Day 3

Wine and lunch: a wonderful tasting of the world-famous Brunello di Montalcino with
Gianluca and his wife Kai at Santa Giulia. Then, we will have lunch at the winery with a
delicious home cooked meal served up by Nona Mirella, Gianluca's mom.

A little culture: a visit to the gardens of nearby La Foce, a mini paradise. What will be in
bloom this month?

### Day 4

A little culture: a guided tour of medieval Siena (or wander the streets on your own if you
prefer).

Wine tasting: we will meet up with Andrea of I Sodi winery at his family's incredible
vineyard in the historic Chianti Classico district. Most people say this is their favorite
winery visit of the tour, and that is really saying something!

Eating: we will have a lunch and wine in Siena at Pretto. If you like, back in Montepulciano,
we will arrange traditional Tuscan soup and some wine with friend Cristina.

### Day 5

A little culture: this is a relaxing afternoon to explore the charming village of Pienza. You
may even consider purchasing your next residence in Pienza after spending an afternoon here.

Eating: we are going to have dinner as a group tonight in Montepulciano.

### Day 6

Cooking class, lunch and wine tasting: we are going to have a cooking class and lunch at a
stunning location facing the largest mountain in this part of Tuscany.

### Day 7: Drop off in Florence

We will drop you off in Florence (at the airport or the train station Santa Maria Novella) at
around noon for you to continue your Italian adventure.
