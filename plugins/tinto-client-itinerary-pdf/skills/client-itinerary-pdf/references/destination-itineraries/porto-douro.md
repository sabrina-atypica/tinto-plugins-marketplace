## Cover copy

Porto is arguably one of Europe's most exciting mid-sized cities today: vibrant, historic,
and irresistibly delicious. Together, we'll dive deep into its rich cultural fabric, exploring
the city's unique mix of architecture, visiting legendary Port wine lodges across the river in
Vila Nova de Gaia, and discovering the exceptional food, wine, and traditions that make
northern Portugal so special.

From there, we'll venture upriver to the breathtaking Douro Valley, one of the world's oldest
demarcated wine regions. Picture steep terraced vineyards cascading down to the river,
jaw-dropping panoramas at every turn, exceptional wines, and the region's soulful, traditional
cuisine. It's every bit as magical as it looks.

Join us for an unforgettable immersion into the heart and soul of northern Portugal.

3 nights in Porto and 3 nights in the Douro Valley. $5,300 per person (double occupancy), or
$6,300 (single occupancy). There will be a total of 7 wine experiences on this tour, including
vintage ports, tawny ports and a range of high end white and red Douro wines.

Porto is a feast for the senses, old world Europe at its finest. Naturally beautiful with steep
banks rising from the Douro River. The Atlantic Ocean is a mere mile away. The historical
center is compact, we will walk most places. There are so many historical sights to enjoy, the
question is how best to spend our limited time.

And the food. The Portuguese love to eat. Traditional food, lots of meat, lots of fish, simply
prepared using the highest quality ingredients.

Coupled with some of the best red wine you may drink this year, plus incredibly unique and
great white wines, makes this a trip for the senses without taking into account Port wine. If
you don't drink Port on a regular basis, you will learn a bit and wind up with a Port wine
habit after the trip. If you already enjoy Port, prepare yourself for the gift of some highly
sought after "colheitas".

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: An introduction to Porto and the grapes of the Douro Valley

Culture, wine and food: begin your journey with the perfect introduction to Porto, a city
where centuries of history, remarkable architecture, and an incredible food culture come
together. We'll explore the historic center with our favorite local guide, Pedro, whose passion
and humor bring the city to life. Afterwards, we'll settle in for a leisurely lunch accompanied
by an introduction to the wines of northern Portugal at one of our favorite local restaurants.

### Day 2: The wine region of Bairrada

Today we venture south to the fascinating wine region of Bairrada, one of Portugal's
best-kept secrets. You'll discover distinctive indigenous grape varieties such as Baga and
Bical while visiting an exceptional local producer. No visit to Bairrada would be complete
without enjoying its most famous culinary tradition, succulent roast suckling pig, considered
by many to be the finest in Portugal.

### Day 3: The Bolsa and a visit to a Port wine lodge

Today blends history, coastal scenery, and one of Portugal's most iconic wines. We'll begin
with a visit to the magnificent Palácio da Bolsa before enjoying lunch overlooking the
Atlantic Ocean. In the afternoon, we'll cross the Douro River into Vila Nova de Gaia, where
generations of Port producers have aged their wines. There, we'll tour one of our favorite Port
lodges and enjoy a guided tasting of this legendary wine.

### Day 4: A private boat ride up the Douro, but first a wine tasting

The Douro Valley really is as beautiful as it looks. One of the oldest demarcated wine
regions in the world. Steep hillside terraces. Drop dead views. Incredible wines, rustic fare.
We will spend three days eating and drinking, while exploring both the power and finesse of
Douro wines. The Douro is an approximately 90 minute drive from Porto, and we will head
there this morning.

After a scenic drive into the heart of the Douro Valley, we'll begin with a visit to a boutique
winery near Pinhão, where you'll be introduced to the elegant still wines that have made the
region internationally renowned. From there, we'll board our private boat for a leisurely
cruise along the Douro River, enjoying Portuguese tapas and wines as terraced vineyards
unfold around every bend. Along the way, we'll also discover the fresh, vibrant wines of
nearby Vinho Verde.

We are staying in the heart of the valley near the town of Peso da Régua.

### Day 5: Quinta do Tourais and the town of Lamego

This morning we'll visit the historic town of Lamego, home to one of Portugal's most
beautiful Baroque sanctuaries. Afterwards, we'll spend time with our dear friends Fernando
and Liliana at their family owned winery, Quinta de Tourais, where we'll taste their
exceptional wines paired with traditional Portuguese petiscos. We'll end the day with dinner
at one of our favorite local restaurants, owned by a small wine producer in Peso da Régua.

### Day 6: A final taste of the Douro

We'll conclude our Douro experience with a visit to one of the region's most respected
wineries, celebrated throughout Portugal for producing outstanding red wines. A leisurely
tasting and farewell lunch provide the perfect ending to a week of unforgettable food, wine,
and friendship.

### Day 7: Boa Viagem

After breakfast, we'll depart the Douro Valley and return to Porto, where we'll drop you at
either the Porto airport or the city's main train station. If you have a different destination
planned, please let us know and we can help you arrange transport.
