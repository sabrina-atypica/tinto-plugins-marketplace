## Cover copy

Travel to Northern Italy & Slovenia with Mike. Exploring the Northern Adriatic, from Verona
to Trieste, and diving deeper into Slovenia, from "Little Tuscany" to Ljubljana. Pick up in
Milan and drop off in Venice.

Northern Italy is different from the rest of the country. The biggest differences in Italy are
from North to South rather than East to West. If you have been, you probably think about
returning. If you haven't, this is a very diverse, and special, part of Italy.

Exceptional natural beauty: the largest Italian lake fed from Alpine streams, the Dolomite
mountains in the distance, the undulating hills of the Italian Slovenian border, the Adriatic
sea.

Culture: quaint lakeside villages, the obligatory Roman villa, the architecture of Palladio,
the charming sea facing Trieste and the beauty of Verona.

The food: risotto, gnocchi, carpaccio, polenta, the home of Grana Padano and steak that is
better than Tuscany!

And the wine. A real treat for wine lovers. New varieties galore. Marzemino, gropello,
schioppettino, refosco, vespaiolo. Have you tried a great Amarone? A new variety each day. Is
Franciacorta better than champagne? You decide.

Slovenia is a special place. Natural beauty, nice people who all seem to speak English, plus a
food and wine scene that should be on the world map. Influences from all around, from Italy to
Central Europe.

We have arranged for a hotel near the Milan central train station where we will stay together
the first night. For our next three nights, we will be based in the charming village of
Sirmione on the shores of Lake Garda. One day we will visit the Roman city of Verona, whose
Colosseum will surprise you. Another day we will take a boat ride on Lake Garda and visit a
local family run winery. We will also visit the most prestigious sparkling wine region in Italy
and eat at one of our favorite trattorias in the entire country.

For our next 3 nights, we will be based in the city of Trieste along the Adriatic Sea. With
perhaps the best coffee culture in Italy, we will spend our days exploring the region along the
Slovenian and Italian coast. You will be wowed by the natural beauty, the incredible food and
some of the most interesting wines you can drink today.

We then make our way to the capital of Slovenia, Ljubljana, for our next 3 nights. One of the
more charming small European capitals, we will eat and drink like kings for our next 3 days
together.

Finally, we will make our way to Venice with the drop off at the train station in Mestre
followed by the Venice airport. From there, you can continue your journey to Venice via a
water taxi.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: Monday, Milan

You will be tired from your flight. Enjoy the bustle of Milan and get a good night's sleep. We
have a lot to see and do over the coming days.

### Day 2: Tuesday, Franciacorta and heading to Sirmione

Wine focus: the sparkling wine region of Franciacorta. Leave prosecco behind for today, this
is by far the best sparkling wine region in Italy.

Food focus: we will have lunch in the small town of Rovata at perhaps our favorite little
restaurant in this part of Italy.

Stay: our hotel is on the shores of Lake Garda in the charming village of Sirmione.

### Day 3: Wednesday, Valpolicella

Culture: we are going to have a walking tour to Verona this morning and of course see its
majestic Colosseum.

Food focus: this neighborhood trattoria near the winery is strictly locals only.

Wine focus: Fattori is a family winery. Today you will learn the difference between
Valpolicella, Ripasso and Amarone. Did we mention their suave is exceptional.

### Day 4: Thursday, Largo di Garda

Culture: we are going to take a private boat ride across the lake.

Wine and food focus: from there, we will make our way to lunch and then off to the Turina
family winery where you get to try at least 3 grape varieties you may not have experienced
previously, marzemino, groppelo and the white Lugana from the trebbiano grape.

### Day 5: Friday, transition day through Bassano del Grapo

It's a longer day as we make our way to Trieste, but certain to be a delicious one.

Culture, wine and food focus: we are going to have lunch with at least one of the five sisters
and visit the Palladian villa and winery of Villa Angarano.

Arrival: we will arrive at Trieste where you are free to wander this evening.

### Day 6: Saturday, Trieste, Austria Hungary along the Adriatic

Culture: Trieste is Italian, right? With probably the best café culture in all of Italy, a true
crossroads town right on the Adriatic. We will begin the day after breakfast with a walking
tour.

Wine focus: Parovel is a small winery in the Karst, with Slovenia just beyond the hills.
Refosco is a great grape to add to your repertoire.

Food focus: we will have lunch together in Trieste.

### Day 7: Sunday, Mediterranean Slovenia

Culture: we are going to visit the picture-perfect Castle of Stanjel in Slovenia today.

Wine and food focus: we will have lunch together near the castle with a mother, daughter team
and then make our way to a wine tasting where you will receive a proper introduction to
Slovenian wine.

### Day 8: Monday, heading to Ljubljana with a magnificent lunch along the way

We head inland today, and you will likely see the snowcapped mountains in the distance.

Culture: on our way to Ljubljana we will stop in a pretty little village for what might be some
of the best food currently in Slovenia.

Arrival: we will arrive at our hotel in the center of Ljubljana where you can wander on your
own in the evening.

### Day 9: Tuesday, Ljubljana, a Slovenian secret

We will do a walking tour of the capital in the morning before making our way to lunch at a
restaurant near the Tivoli gardens.

Wine focus: this evening, we will have an easy, informal get together trying some of the other
Slovenian wine regions more inland at a local wine bar, a short walk from the hotel.

### Day 10: Wednesday, Alpine beauty

A little culture: we will head toward the Austrian border before stopping to see a stunning
Alpine lake.

Food focus: we are going to have an exceptional lunch in a tiny town that we found out about
from a Slovenian friend. From there, we will have a coffee and digestif at a spot where you can
then walk to enjoy the lake.

### Day 11: Thursday, heading to Venice

This morning, we will make our way to Venice.
