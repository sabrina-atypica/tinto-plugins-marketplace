## Cover copy

Inaugural Christmas Markets & Festive Wine Tour. Munich, Salzburg, Vienna. 7 nights.

Join Big Cork for their very first Christmas Markets Tour, a festive journey through three of
Europe's most enchanting cities during the most magical season of the year. Imagine strolling
through centuries-old Christmas markets illuminated by thousands of twinkling lights,
sipping mulled wine beneath Gothic cathedrals, discovering charming villages, tasting
exceptional Austrian wines, and sharing unforgettable meals with fellow travelers. This tour
combines everything we love: wonderful food, boutique wineries, authentic local experiences,
and just the right amount of holiday magic.

Why you'll love this tour: the Christmas markets of Bavaria and Austria are considered
among the finest in the world. Each city has its own personality and traditions, from
handcrafted ornaments and roasted chestnuts to warm gingerbread, artisan gifts, and steaming
mugs of Glühwein.

Limited to 20 participants. Single travelers welcome. Single supplement: $1,400.

Tour begins in Munich and concludes in Vienna. Most flights from the United States to
Germany are overnight flights. The tour begins at 4 pm on Sunday. If you would like to arrive
earlier, our travel partners at Tinto Travels can assist you. The airport in Vienna is
approximately 25 minutes from the city center.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: Sunday, December 5, welcome to Munich

Welcome to Bavaria! After checking into the beautiful 5-star Charles Hotel, you'll have time
to relax before meeting your fellow travelers.

In the late afternoon, we'll make our first visit to Munich's world-famous Christmas Market.
Wander among the stalls while enjoying your first taste of the festive season with traditional
holiday treats and perhaps your first mug of warm Glühwein.

This evening we'll gather for our Welcome Dinner and toast the beginning of a special week
together.

### Day 2: Monday, December 6, Munich to Salzburg

After breakfast, we journey into Austria and the storybook city of Salzburg.

Following lunch in the historic center, we'll settle into our centrally located hotel before
discovering one of Austria's more charming Christmas Markets.

This evening we'll enjoy a wine tasting at one of our favorite local wine bars before soaking
in the festive atmosphere of Salzburg after dark.

### Day 3: Tuesday, December 7, Salzburg & the home of Mozart

Today begins with a guided walking tour through Salzburg's beautifully preserved Old Town.
You'll discover Mozart's birthplace, magnificent Baroque architecture, hidden squares, and
centuries of fascinating history.

Lunch features one of Austria's greatest culinary classics, a proper Wiener Schnitzel.

The remainder of the evening is yours to explore Salzburg's Christmas markets, cafés and
cozy wine bars at your leisure.

### Day 4: Wednesday, December 8, to Vienna via Linz

Today we travel east toward Vienna, stopping along the way in the picturesque riverside city
of Linz on the Danube.

After enjoying lunch, we'll continue to Vienna, our home for the next four nights.

This evening we'll stroll through one of Vienna's spectacular Christmas Markets, where the
imperial city's holiday atmosphere truly comes alive.

### Day 5: Thursday, December 9, discovering Vienna

This morning we'll enjoy a guided walking tour through one of Europe's most elegant
capitals. From grand boulevards and magnificent architecture to hidden courtyards and local
neighborhoods, Vienna effortlessly blends history with contemporary sophistication.

Lunch will be at one of our favorite restaurants across the river.

Later this evening we'll gather for another boutique wine tasting featuring outstanding
Austrian wines at a local wine bar.

### Day 6: Friday, December 10, the Kamptal wine region

Today we venture into the beautiful Kamptal wine region. We'll visit one of our favorite
family owned wineries, where a wonderful husband and wife team produces exceptional wines
while sharing their passion for the region.

Lunch will be served in a postcard-perfect Austrian village before returning to Vienna.

This evening is yours to enjoy. We highly recommend visiting the Christmas Market beside the
magnificent Belvedere Palace. Art lovers may also wish to admire Gustav Klimt's
masterpieces, an experience worth traveling to Vienna for on its own.

### Day 7: Saturday, December 11, the Gols wine region

Our final full day takes us to the renowned wine region of Gols. We'll visit a
fourth-generation organic and biodynamic winery where tradition and innovation merge
seamlessly.

Following another leisurely lunch in a nearby village, we'll return to Vienna.

Spend your final evening exactly as you wish, perhaps one last visit to your favorite
Christmas Market, a concert, or simply enjoying a glass of Austrian wine while reflecting on
a memorable week together.

### Day 8: Sunday, December 12, Auf Wiedersehen

After breakfast, our Christmas Markets adventure comes to an end. Vienna International
Airport is approximately 25 minutes from the city center.

Until next time. Frohe Weihnachten.
