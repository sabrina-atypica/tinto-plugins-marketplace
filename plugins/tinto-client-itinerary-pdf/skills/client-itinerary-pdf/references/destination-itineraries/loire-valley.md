## Cover copy

Come fall in love with the Loire Valley. This is an exceptional tour for the foodie, the wine
lover or anyone with an interest in seeing a few of the iconic chateaux of France.

You will try incredible variations of Chenin blanc, Cabernet Franc and a few other special
varietals in the Loire, from Sauvignon blanc to Gamay. We will try to track down some Melon
de Bourgogne. These are ideal food wines.

All the restaurants we visit are family owned. The Loire is the true breadbasket of France. The
menus change daily, but you can be assured we will eat and drink like the royalty that has
called the Loire home for a millennia.

A day with us: a day on our tour consists of a little culture, a wine tasting and either a long
leisurely lunch or dinner. Our start time in the morning is generally 10am. We do not visit
multiple wineries in a single day.

6 nights in the Loire Valley, pick up and drop off in Paris. We love Paris. It is a vibrant,
busy city and probably best explored on your own or in very small groups. If you would like to
extend your trip or arrive early, we are happy to provide you with hotel recommendations and
things to see and do. Please plan to arrive in Paris no later than the Sunday before the tour
starts.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: Monday, heading to the Loire, via Orléans

We will depart from a convenient central Paris location, with easy access out of the city. The
drive to Tours takes approximately three hours.

Along the way, we'll stop in Orléans for lunch at a family-run restaurant where young chefs
Marine and Gauthier will treat us to a fresh, inventive menu perfectly paired with local wines.

After lunch, enjoy a guided visit to Château de Chambord, one of the Loire's most iconic
landmarks and a masterpiece of French Renaissance architecture.

We'll then arrive in the dynamic town of Tours, which will be our base for the next three
nights. Spend the evening exploring the historic center and discovering all the delights it has
to offer.

### Day 2: Tuesday, a city tour & a French cooking class in Tours

We'll begin our day with a walking tour of Tours. Our guide, Celine, will bring the city's
history, streets, and hidden gems to life.

A short stroll through Les Halles de Tours, the city's vibrant indoor food market, will allow us
to pick up fresh ingredients. Then we'll head to Caroline's kitchen, where she and her team
will guide us in preparing some classic French dishes. We promise, this will be the most fun
cooking class you've ever had!

Lunch will be the delicious reward of our culinary efforts, naturally accompanied by wines of
the region.

You will have your evenings off to explore all the great shopping, sightseeing, and culinary
treats Tours has to offer.

### Day 3: Wednesday, Chinon and the expression of different soils

Today we make our way west to Chinon, the appellation known for the Loire's most diverse
expressions of Cabernet Franc: from light and fruity to medium-bodied and peppery, and even
deeply structured wines capable of aging. Soil makes all the difference here.

We will visit and taste beautiful reds and some surprising whites in a stunning location.
Shhhh, the location is still a secret!

If the weather is on our side, we will enjoy a boat ride on the Loire River from the serene and
beautiful village of Candes-Saint-Martin.

### Day 4: Thursday, Chateau de Minière & home cooking with Vincent

Today we will leave Tours and make our way to the quaint town of Amboise. But first we make
a stop in Bourgueil where we will visit the centuries old Chateau de Minière, a standout
winery producing expressive Cabernet Francs, a hallmark of the region.

Afterwards we will have lunch at Vincent, the Cuisinier de Campagne. Vincent runs a small
restaurant and farm together with his wife. They will host us in his welcoming countryside
home. Expect a memorable lunch crafted with care and warmth, served in his dining room.

We will continue to the picturesque town of Amboise, once home to Leonardo da Vinci. After
checking in to our hotel, enjoy a free evening to wander its charming streets and soak in the
atmosphere.

### Day 5: Friday, Vouvray & a favorite château

Staying close to Amboise today, we'll begin with a guided tour of Château de Chenonceau led
by the delightful Maria Massari. This chateau, elegantly spanning the River Cher, is one of the
most enchanting in the Loire.

From there, we enter Vouvray territory to meet Peter Hahn at Le Clos de la Meslerie, a
micro-estate in the heart of the appellation. Peter will share his journey from the world of
finance to life as a Loire Valley winegrower. You will taste exceptional expressions of Chenin
Blanc. Lunch will be lovingly prepared by his wife, Juliette.

In the evening, return to Amboise for a relaxed wine tasting and a touch of local French
entertainment at the hotel. The rest of the evening is yours to enjoy.

### Day 6: Saturday, Touraine & off the beaten path

Today we venture off the beaten path to the charming and less-touristy town of Loches, where
we'll explore the weekly Saturday market brimming with local color.

Lunch awaits at the cheerful Au Comptoir, right in the heart of Loches.

In the evening enjoy the pace and scale of Amboise, where friendly cafés and restaurants line
the street with a view to the chateau of Amboise.

### Day 7: Sunday, bon voyage

This morning, we head back to Paris. Drop-offs will be at Charles de Gaulle Airport and at our
central Paris pick up location.

Important: if flying out today, please make note that arrival at the airport will be at 12 noon.
