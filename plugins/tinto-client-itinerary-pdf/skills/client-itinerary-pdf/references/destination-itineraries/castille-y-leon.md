## Cover copy

An insider's view of the heart of Spain, Castille & León and the Ribera del Duero.

Discover the beautiful UNESCO medieval towns of Ávila and Segovia set in the mountains, 70
miles northwest of Madrid, in the region of Castilla y León. We will use the walled town of
Ávila as a base for our first three nights while we discover the red wines of San Martin de
Valdeiglesias and the whites from Rueda. We visit Segovia with its 1st century, 3,000 foot
aqueduct and experience a true "cochinillo" gastronomic experience.

For the second half of our tour, we will stay at the magnificent town of Burgos, which was the
capital of Castille y León for 5 centuries. It is most known for its stunning Gothic cathedral
and is also on the Camino de Santiago. We will do a day trip to the heart of Ribera del Duero.
Tempranillo is the grape of this region, and we will taste some of the most elegant, full
bodied red the region has to offer.

Pick up and drop off in Madrid. Single travelers welcome. If you prefer a single room,
additional charge is $800.

Madrid is a vibrant city that cannot be missed. Madrid's "Big 3" museums warrant a trip on
their own. You must see Picasso's Guernica at the Reina Sofia. If you would like to extend
your trip or arrive early, we are happy to assist with hotel recommendations and things to see
and do. Please plan to arrive in Madrid no later than the Sunday prior to the tour start.

## Day-by-day

### Day 1: Madrid to Ávila

A little culture: the focus today is on Rafael and his small vineyard. We should arrive in
Ávila with just enough time to see the sun settle over perhaps the best-preserved medieval
walls of Europe. Our hotel is located inside the city walls and has a lovely outdoor terrace.

Wine focus: you have hopefully never seen vines as old as the ones at Tierra Calma.

Food focus: Rafael is going to prepare his famous paella for us.

### Day 2: Segovia

A little culture: Segovia has this magical feel.

Wine focus: the primary focus today is on the pairings with local food.

Food focus: this restaurant is an institution and prepares the best "cochinillo asado." It may
take a while to get hungry again.

### Day 3: Escurial and the Royal Family

A little culture: actually, it is a lot of culture. The Monastery and Site of the Escorial
represents the Golden Age of Spanish expansion.

Food and wine: we are going to have lunch in the village of Escorial today. You are welcome
to wander around Ávila in the evening.

### Day 4: Ávila to Burgos, via Rueda

A little culture: the Roman villa was "discovered" fairly recently by a farmer. The mosaics
will surprise you in their level of detail and preservation.

Wine focus: forget about language barriers, Miguel makes you feel immediately comfortable
in his presence. His wines are delicious and unlike mass produced Verdejo.

Food focus: we are going to eat at a local restaurant in Miguel's village today. We will have
trouble saying goodbye at the end of the afternoon.

### Day 5: Burgos

A little culture: we will do a leisurely walking tour of Burgos today, including a visit to the
famous Burgos cathedral. We will learn about the Camino de Santiago and El Cid.

Wine focus: resurrecting the DO of Arlanza. Andres Septien does it all at his tiny winery.
Maybe one day he will give up his day job.

Food focus: the perfect pairing! Mexico meets Spain. Some people say this is the best food of
the tour. Fernando is originally from Mexico and Edurne from Burgos. The things you do for
love.

### Day 6: Ribera del Duero

Culture: the old wine cellar of the Epifania family will surprise you.

Wine focus: the three brothers Epifania have been making wine for a long while. The wines
are balanced and smooth, exactly what one wants from a fine Tempranillo. With a little luck,
we will sample wines directly from the tank.

Food focus: we are going to have lechazo, the culinary prize of the Ribera del Duero, for
lunch today.

### Day 7: Ciao for now

We will head back to Madrid in the morning after breakfast. We can arrive at Barajas airport
by 11am and then follow to Atocha train station. If you plan to fly back to the states on
Sunday, please make your flight reservation for after 1:30pm.
